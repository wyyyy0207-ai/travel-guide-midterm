Destinations

Get to know four cities through their sights, buildings, and food.

Beijing | Shanghai | Chengdu | Xi'an

Beijing

Beijing has both historic sights and a modern skyline. Visit Tiananmen and the Forbidden City, then see the tall buildings around Guomao and the CBD.

Tiananmen

Tiananmen is a well-known landmark in Beijing. This picture shows the red walls and the lights on the gate at night.

Guomao

Guomao is part of modern Beijing. Tall buildings behind traditional rooftops show the old and new sides of the city.

Beijing CBD

The Central Business District, or CBD, has many tall office buildings. Its busy roads and evening lights show a lively city scene.

The Forbidden City

The Forbidden City shows the historic side of Beijing. Its palace buildings and tiled roofs look warm in the evening light.

Shanghai

Shanghai is an important economic and financial center in China. Its modern skyline, the Bund, and Shanghai Disneyland show different sides of the city.

Lujiazui at Night

Lujiazui has many modern skyscrapers. The city lights and the Huangpu River show the busy, modern side of Shanghai.

The Oriental Pearl Tower and the Huangpu River

The Oriental Pearl Tower stands beside the Huangpu River. Across the river, the Bund has historic buildings along the waterfront.

The Lujiazui Skyline at Sunset

The Bund is a place to enjoy views of the Huangpu River and the Lujiazui skyline. At sunset, the buildings stand against a pink sky.

Shanghai Disneyland

Shanghai Disneyland is a theme park for a day out with family or friends. This picture shows its castle and a reflection in the water.

Chengdu

Chengdu offers city views, panda culture, riverside scenery in Dujiangyan, and Sichuan hotpot. It is a city with plenty to see and taste.

Chengdu City View

Chengdu has modern buildings and green spaces along the river. This city view shows its skyline and riverside lights.

Panda Culture

Pandas are a symbol of Chengdu. This playful panda sculpture shows how panda-themed decorations are part of the city.

Dujiangyan at Night

Dujiangyan has riverside scenery and traditional architecture. At night, warm lights on the buildings contrast with the blue light on the water.

Sichuan Hotpot

Sichuan hotpot is a spicy meal to share with friends. Meat and vegetables are cooked in a hot broth and served with dipping sauces.

Xi'an

Xi'an brings history and modern city life together. Explore its city wall, Tang-style night scenes, Terracotta Warriors, and traditional architecture.

Xi'an: Old and New

Xi'an brings old and new together. This picture shows the city wall and traditional-style towers with modern buildings in the distance.

Grand Tang Mall at Night

Grand Tang Mall is a pedestrian area with Tang-style buildings and colorful lights. Its lanterns and night scenes create a lively atmosphere.

The Terracotta Warriors

The Terracotta Warriors are an important part of Xi'an's history. Rows of clay soldiers and horses make this archaeological site impressive.

Traditional Architecture

Layered roofs, painted details, and red columns show traditional Chinese architectural style. This picture highlights the details of the buildings.
