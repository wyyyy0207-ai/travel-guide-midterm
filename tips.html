Travel Tips

A few simple ideas to help you prepare for your trip.

1. Make a Simple Plan

Choose the places you want to see and group nearby sights together. Leave some free time instead of filling every hour.

  • Check the opening times of attractions before you go.
  • Find out whether a ticket or reservation is needed.
  • Save your hotel address and important travel details.

2. Pack the Essentials

  • ID or passport and travel documents
  • Phone, charger, and a power bank
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A water bottle and an umbrella
  • Clothes suitable for the weather

3. Think About Transport

Check a map before leaving your accommodation. Public transport can be useful for city trips. Keep enough time for the journey between sights.

4. Enjoy Local Food and Culture

Try food that interests you and ask about ingredients if you need to. For hotpot, choose a spice level you feel comfortable with.

Follow the rules at museums and historic places. Keep public spaces clean and be considerate when taking photos.
